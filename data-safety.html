Data collection and security

  • Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? Yes
  • Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? Yes
  • Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? Yes — in-app via Settings → Delete account, plus by emailing muhammad.jawwad.dev@gmail.com. Deleted from the backend immediately; hosting-provider backups expire within 30 days.
  • Data deletion URL: see the privacy policy
  • Account creation method: OAuth
